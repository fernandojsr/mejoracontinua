Platform-specific tactics

Same story everywhere, different mechanics per platform.

Li

LinkedIn

Primary / global
  • Set "Open to Work" tags: Project Manager, IT Coordinator, Digital Transformation Consultant, Training & Development
  • Lead About section with the transformation story, not a chronological list
  • Turn on remote + part-time filters in job search settings
  • Daily alert 1 — Digital Transformation Consultant (DACH: Bavaria + Germany + Remote-Germany combined; German/English/Spanish as a keyword modifier, not a separate search)
  • Daily alert 2 — IT Project Manager (DACH, includes IT Operations/Implementation keywords as secondary terms; same trilingual modifier layered in)
  • Weekly sweep 3 — Corporate Trainer / L&D — DACH
  • Weekly sweep 4 — E-Learning Coordinator
  • Trilingual Technical Project Coordinator — retired as a standalone lane; "trilingual" undersells seniority as a title but works as a differentiator on searches 1 & 2
  • Spain — location filter added to searches 1 & 2 only, reviewed weekly (not a primary market yet)
  • AI Transformation/Implementation — experimental, manual check once a month, no standing alert
  • Verify current alert limits directly in account settings before relying on all 4 as live alerts — if capped, keep 1 & 2 as alerts and run 3 & 4 manually
EU

EURES

Relocation track
  • Create a Europass profile and publish it directly to the EURES portal
  • Book a session with an EURES adviser for relocation + matching support
  • Best for Track B (relocation), less useful for remote-only search
Xi

Xing

DACH / Mittelstand
  • Complete the German-language profile fully — signals local commitment
  • Trusted by recruiters at mid-size German firms more than LinkedIn
  • Use "Home Office" and "Teilzeit" filters in Stellenmarkt
In

Indeed & Stepstone

Volume + Minijob
  • Best for volume and Minijob/Teilzeit listings specifically
  • Save searches in German too: Projektmanager, Digitale Transformation, IT-Koordinator
  • Add "Homeoffice" and "Minijob" as explicit saved-search terms
Rm

Remote boards

Arbeitnow · Jobgether · Working Nomads
  • Arbeitnow tracks 100% remote German roles and flags Minijob/Werkstudent listings
  • Jobgether curates remote roles specifically for senior/experienced professionals
  • Working Nomads / EU Remote Jobs / NoDesk for a wider European net
Tu

Tutoring

Preply · iTalki
  • Fastest to start — can go live within days
  • Leverages 14 years of lecturing experience directly
  • Good short-term bridge while Track B ramps up
